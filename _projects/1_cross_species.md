**Collaboration with Daniel Meyer (Knowles Lab), Columbia University** · Sep 2024 – May 2025

Comparing single-cell data across species is hard because gene expression spaces don't line up one-to-one. In this project, we built a graph neural network–based variational autoencoder (GNN-VAE) in PyTorch to align cross-species scRNA-seq datasets into a shared latent space, enabling comparative genomics analysis at scale.

- Designed and implemented the GNN-VAE model in PyTorch
- Developed a benchmarking framework with standardized metrics and evaluation protocols
- Presented the work as a poster (second author) at [Machine Learning in Computational Biology (MLCB) 2025](https://www.mlcb.org/)
