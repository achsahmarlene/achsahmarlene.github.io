I'm a Bioinformatician III in the Programmable Genomics Lab (PI: Dr. Jacob Schreiber) at UMass Chan Medical School, where I'm designing synthetic regulatory elements for different disease conditions in various cell types. My work lies at the intersection of machine learning, genomics, and computational biology.

I completed my Master of Science in Biomedical Engineering at Columbia University in December 2025, with a focus on Bioinformatics and Machine Learning. At Columbia, I worked on spatial genomics integration in the [Azizi Lab](https://www.azizilab.com/) (PI: Dr. Elham Azizi), and collaborated with the Knowles Lab on a [cross-species data integration project](/projects/1_cross_species/) using graph neural networks, which we presented as a poster at MLCB 2025. I'm passionate about building scalable ML pipelines and multi-omics integration frameworks that enable biological discovery.

My technical expertise spans Python, R, PyTorch and bioinformatics tools like ScanPy and ArchR. I have experience developing deep learning models for genomic data, including graph neural network-based variational autoencoders for cross-species alignment and hypergraph-based architectures for multimodal data integration.

Prior to Columbia, I completed my Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech) in Mechanical Engineering at the [Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Ropar](https://www.iitrpr.ac.in/), where I worked on computational modeling of biomechanical systems and developed models for mechanotransduction signaling.

I've also had the opportunity to teach graduate-level courses in machine learning, deep learning, and computational genomics at Columbia.
